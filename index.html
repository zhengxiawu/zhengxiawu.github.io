Biography

I am an Associate Professor and PhD supervisor at the Institute of Artificial Intelligence, Xiamen University, a jointly appointed Associate Researcher at Peng Cheng Laboratory, and an IEEE Senior Member. I received my PhD from Xiamen University, advised by Prof. Rongrong Ji, and was a postdoctoral researcher at Peng Cheng Laboratory from 2022 to 2023, working with Prof. Yonghong Tian. My research focuses on automated machine learning, neural architecture search, model compression and efficient large-model deployment. I have published 25 CCF-A papers as first or corresponding author. My work has received over 13,000 citations on Google Scholar and has been cited multiple times in technical reports for Google's Gemini, Microsoft's Phi and Alibaba's Qwen. I lead an NSFC General Program project and multiple industry-sponsored projects with partners including Huawei, HiSilicon and ByteDance. The resulting technologies have been applied to products at Huawei, HiSilicon, Tencent, ByteDance, Alibaba/Tmall, HONOR, VIVO, OPPO and DiDi. I have received support from the Postdoctoral Innovative Talent Support Program, the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation and the Xiamen University Xiaomi Young Scholar Program, and collaborate on open-source research including MetaGPT with Jürgen Schmidhuber. I previously served on secondment in the Software Division of the Xiamen Municipal Bureau of Industry and Information Technology, contributing to AI innovation-center planning, industry development and policy materials. I teach Algorithm Design and Analysis and Art and Technology.
